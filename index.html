Not AI — on purpose

Built for people who won't
outsource their thinking.

VisiDocsverse contains no generative AI, no chatbot, no summarizer. Nothing is paraphrased, guessed, or “helpfully” rewritten. The app maps what your documents actually share — tags, topics, references — with deterministic rules, so the same files always produce the same universe. You read. You connect. You decide. The software just makes the relationships visible so your judgment gets sharper instead of replaced.

🧭 What VisiDocsverse does instead

  • Shows every source in place. Each document stays whole, readable, and clickable at the center of its own neighborhood.
  • Links you can verify. Every tether is shared ground you can open — a tag, a topic, a reference — not a generated claim.
  • Same files → same map, every time. Deterministic physics. Reopen tomorrow and the constellation is where you left it.
  • Thinking aid, not answer machine. It organizes the evidence; the interpretation stays yours.

⚠️ Why AI is an unreliable partner for documents

  • Ask twice, get two different answers. The same set of documents can produce a different synthesis on every run — which version do you cite?
  • Sources flattened into a paragraph. Summaries bury the document you needed to actually read, and confident phrasing hides the gaps.
  • Citations you still have to check. A fluent answer with a wrong page number costs more time than finding the passage yourself.
  • Your files leave the room. Cloud models upload, retain, or train on what you paste — a non-starter for theses, cases, and client work.

Questions, answered

FAQ

What exactly does VisiDocsverse do with my documents?

It turns each file into a body in a 3D physics space. Documents that share tags, topics, filename concepts or explicit references pull toward each other with spring tethers; heavier documents (more words + references) pull harder. You read any file at the center with everything related orbiting it.

Which formats can I import?

PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx/.xls, per-sheet), PowerPoint (.pptx), Markdown, plain text, CSV, video (.mp4/.mov/.webm), audio (.mp3/.wav/.m4a/.ogg), and photos (.jpg/.png/.gif/.webp). Embedded pictures import too, and photographed text is OCR'd locally so it tags, tethers and searches like typed text.

Does it upload my files anywhere?

No. Everything — parsing, OCR, search, storage — runs on your PC. The only network calls are license activation, a weekly license check (with 7-day offline grace), and update notices.

Does VisiDocsverse use AI?

No — deliberately. There is no chatbot, no summarizer, no generative model anywhere in the app. Mapping is deterministic: the same documents always produce the same universe, every link is shared ground you can open and verify, and the interpretation stays yours. That is the point — it is built for people who don't want to outsource their thinking. See Not AI — on purpose.

How is this different from Obsidian / Notion / Zotero?

Those are brilliant filing cabinets. VisiDocsverse is an observatory: instead of lists of names, you get a live map of how your documents relate — ideas as bodies (Thematic), labels as bodies (Tags), plus drag-to-merge tags, passage-pinned notes, and multi-canvas project universes.

What if I have hundreds of files?

That's the point. Box-select, fit-to-screen, distribute-into-orb map mode, per-canvas universes, and the verse-map minimap keep big collections navigable. Mass-ranked seating means the heavyweights anchor the center automatically.
