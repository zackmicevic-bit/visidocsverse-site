Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

VisiDocsverse is an offline-first desktop app. This policy explains what information is — and more importantly is not — collected when you use the app and this website.

1. Your documents stay yours

Every document you open in VisiDocsverse — PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets, slides, notes, images — is parsed, indexed, and stored locally on your own Windows machine. Your files are never uploaded to us, synced to a cloud, used for advertising, used to train models, or viewed by anyone but you. Backups are files you create and hold yourself; restoring them never involves our servers.

2. License verification

To confirm your subscription is active, the app contacts Lemon Squeezy:

  • At activation: the license key you paste is checked against Lemon Squeezy's licensing service.
  • About once a week after that: a lightweight check confirms the subscription is still current. If your machine is offline, a 7-day grace period keeps the app fully working.

These checks transmit your license key and basic subscription status only. They never include your documents, filenames, tags, notes, or anything about what you keep in your vault.

3. Checkout and billing

Payments and subscription management are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. When you subscribe, Lemon Squeezy collects your email, payment details, and order information under its own privacy policy, and shares what we need to issue and support your license (your email and order details). We never see or store your payment card details.

4. Update checks

The app periodically fetches a small public version file to see whether a new release exists. This is an anonymous download — it carries no personal data and nothing about your library.

5. This website

This site (including the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages) has no analytics, no trackers, no cookies, no accounts, and no newsletter forms. Videos and screenshots are plain static files. If you click through to checkout, you leave this site and continue on Lemon Squeezy's website under their policies.

6. Data retention

Because the app keeps your content on your machine, there is nothing on our side to retain or delete. License and order records live with Lemon Squeezy and are kept as long as needed for billing, accounting, and support, under their policies.

7. Your rights

For anything relating to your order, subscription, or the personal data held for licensing and billing — access, correction, deletion, or cancellation — use your Lemon Squeezy order page or reply to any Lemon Squeezy receipt email. Cancelling stops future renewals; it never affects the files on your PC.

8. Children

VisiDocsverse is a general-purpose productivity tool not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Since the app collects nothing at all, there is nothing further to do here.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact support through your Lemon Squeezy order page or by replying to any Lemon Squeezy receipt email.
