2 Top pill · Projects & views

Top pill: projects, views & stats

The top pill runs the big picture: which project universe you're in, and which lens you're seeing it through.

  • Project tabs: keep multiple canvases and switch between them.
  • MAIN view: every document as itself, clustered by shared ground.
  • THEMATIC view: the same corpus re-webbed around ideas.
  • TAGS view + Tags Inventory: your labels as a map you can browse, merge, and rename.
▶ Watch on YouTube →
3 Left pane · Mission control

Left pane: every control, what it does

The left pane is mission control for how your universe looks and feels — brightness, body size, glow, tethers, motion, and textures.

  • Labels & Shade: readable text at any size, plus dark / light canvas mode.
  • Bodies, glow & compress: size the orbs, set the halo, squeeze the layout.
  • Tethers: line thickness plus dotted, sparse, and dashed styles.
  • Rotation, overlays & backdrops: spin, photo textures, and canvas scenery.
▶ Watch on YouTube →
4 Bottom pill · Canvas controls

Bottom pill: Hover Peek, Fit, Tethers & more

The bottom pill is where you drive the canvas itself — preview, arrange, frame, and restyle everything on screen.

  • Hover Peek: preview a document without leaving the map.
  • Distribute & Fit: spread the universe out, or frame it all on screen.
  • Tethers + line styles: show the connections your way.
  • Plain Body & Body To: swap how bodies look — including document icons.
▶ Watch on YouTube →
5 Right side · The reading pane

Reading pane: open, read & work with documents

The right-side pane is where you actually read: click any body and its full text docks beside the universe, ringed by everything it connects to.

  • Open from the canvas — the document glides to the center as the sun.
  • Read & navigate long documents with search hits lit in gold.
  • Body menu: notes, tags, and per-document options.
  • Download the original byte-identical any time; off-click to release.
